Yuxin Lin

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at Caltech, working under Elena Mantovan. I will be an incoming postdoc at the University of Michigan.

I am interested in number theory and algebraic geometry, especially p-divisible groups associated to abelian varieties.

I did my undergraduate studies at the University of Notre Dame.

I can speak Mandarin, English, Japanese, Cantonese and Teochew.

Research

I have conducted research in:

  1. Arithmetic Geometry: I have done projects studying the stratification of moduli spaces of curves by discrete invariants. I am currently doing a project on the structure of the ring of automorphic forms on certain Shimura varieties.
  2. Algebraic Geometry / Coding Theory: I have worked on a project to compute the generalized Hamming weights of projective Reed-Muller codes using techniques in algebraic geometry.
  3. Discrete Math: I have done some projects on statistical questions in number rings, numerical semigroups and their higher dimensional counterparts.
